🏆 Step Challenge

Friendly step challenges with your friends, whatever watch or phone they use.

Step Challenge is a free Android application that reads your daily step count and compares it with your friends' in weekly and monthly leaderboards.

Works with your devices

  • Health Connect on Android: steps counted by your phone or synchronized by compatible watches and apps (Garmin, Samsung, Xiaomi and others).
  • Huawei Health: support for Huawei watches, including on non-Huawei phones, is in progress.

Respectful of your data

  • Free, with no advertising and no tracking.
  • Sign in with Google: only a technical account identifier is kept, never your email address or photo.
  • Only daily step totals are synchronized, nothing else from your health data.
  • Delete your account and all your data at any time from the app's settings.
  • Open source (AGPL-3.0): anyone can check how the app and its server work.

Availability

Step Challenge is currently in closed testing on Google Play.
