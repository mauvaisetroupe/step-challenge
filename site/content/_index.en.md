Step Challenge is a free Android application that reads your daily step
count and compares it with your friends' in weekly and monthly
leaderboards.

## A private leaderboard, between friends

- Invite your friends with a link: only the people you have accepted
  see your steps, and you only see theirs.
- No public ranking, no user search, no directory.

## Works with your devices

- **Health Connect** on Android: steps counted by your phone or
  synchronized by compatible watches and apps (Garmin, Samsung, Xiaomi
  and others). See the [Health Connect help]({{< relref "help/health-connect" >}}).
- **Huawei Health**: support for Huawei watches, including on non-Huawei
  phones, is being validated by Huawei and is not available yet. See the
  [Huawei help]({{< relref "help/huawei" >}}).

## Respectful of your data

- Free, with no advertising and no tracking.
- Sign in with Google: only a technical account identifier is kept,
  never your email address or photo.
- Only daily step totals are synchronized, nothing else from your
  health data.
- Delete your account and all your data at any time from the app's
  settings.
- Open source (AGPL-3.0): anyone can [check how the app and its server
  work](https://github.com/mauvaisetroupe/step-challenge).
