This page explains how to delete your account in **Step Challenge**, the
step challenge application published on Google Play by Lionel Coquin,
and what happens to your data.

## How to delete your account

**From the application** (immediate):

1. Open Step Challenge.
2. Go to the **Settings** tab (*Paramètres*).
3. In the **Account** section (*Compte*), tap **Delete my account**
   (*Supprimer mon compte*).
4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

**Without the application** (for example if you uninstalled it):

1. Send an email to
   [support@architech.lu](mailto:support@architech.lu?subject=Step%20Challenge%20account%20deletion)
   with the subject *"Step Challenge account deletion"*.
2. Send it from the email address of the **Google account you use to
   sign in** to Step Challenge, and include the display name shown in
   the app, so that your account can be identified.
3. Your account is deleted within 30 days, and you receive a
   confirmation by email.

## What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently deletes, from the Step Challenge
server:

- your account and your display name;
- the link between your account and your Google account;
- your sessions (you are signed out on all devices);
- your entire step history;
- your friendships, the nicknames you gave and received, your invitation
  links and your blocks.

## What is kept

- **Nothing linked to your account is kept on the Step Challenge
  server.** No backup copies of the database are retained: deletion is
  immediate and permanent, and cannot be undone.
- **Reports** (if you reported someone, or were reported) are kept,
  without any link to your account, until 12 months after they were
  processed, then deleted. They contain the reported display name and
  the reporter's optional comment.
- Step data stored on your phone by Health Connect or Huawei Health is
  **not** affected: it belongs to those applications, not to Step
  Challenge.
- Your Google account is not affected. You can also remove Step
  Challenge from the apps connected to your Google account in your
  Google account settings.

You can create a new account later by signing in again; it starts with
an empty history, except for the steps that the application reads again
from your phone.
