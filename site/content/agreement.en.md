## 1. About Step Challenge

Step Challenge is a personal fitness and step-counting application
designed to let users track their daily steps, view their activity
history and statistics, and participate in step-based challenges and
leaderboards.

Step Challenge may obtain step data from supported health platforms,
including HUAWEI Health Kit on compatible Huawei devices, when the user
explicitly authorizes access.

## 2. User Account

Some Step Challenge features require a user account or a user
identifier.

You are responsible for providing accurate information when creating or
configuring your account and for using the application only for your
own personal use.

## 3. Health and Step Data

Step Challenge may access daily step counts from a supported health
platform when you grant the required permission.

The application uses step data to provide features such as:

- displaying your daily step count;
- displaying activity history and statistics;
- calculating challenge and leaderboard results;
- synchronizing your step history with the Step Challenge service.

Step Challenge does not require access to unrelated health information
such as heart rate, sleep, blood pressure, or medical records for the
features described above.

Details about the collection, use, storage, and protection of personal
and health-related data are described in the
[Step Challenge Privacy Policy]({{< relref "privacy" >}}).

## 4. Health Platform Permissions

Access to health data is controlled through the permissions provided by
the relevant health platform.

You may refuse or revoke permission to access your health data.
Revoking permission may prevent Step Challenge from updating your step
count or providing features that depend on that data.

or leaderboards.

By participating in a challenge, you understand that information
required to operate the challenge, such as your display name and step
totals, may be visible to other participants according to the
application's functionality.

Challenge results are intended for recreational and fitness purposes.
They are not intended to provide medical, professional, or health
advice.
