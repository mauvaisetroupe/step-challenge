> **Not available yet.** Huawei watch support is being validated by
> Huawei and is **not yet available** in Step Challenge. We cannot say
> when, or whether, it will be. In the meantime, the **Huawei Health**
> section of the app settings does not work. This page will be updated
> when there is news.

## Why Huawei is a special case

Most watches send their steps to Health Connect, where Step Challenge
reads them (see the [Health Connect help]({{< relref "help/health-connect" >}})).
**Huawei Health does not**: its data stays in Huawei's own services.
Step Challenge will therefore have to read your steps from Huawei
directly, through Huawei Health Kit, with your permission.

This page is for people with a **Huawei watch paired with a phone of
another brand** (Samsung, Google Pixel, Xiaomi…), with Google services.

## What it will require

If support becomes available, Huawei's services will need to be
installed on your phone. You can prepare now; these apps are free and
already needed to use a Huawei watch:

1. **AppGallery**, Huawei's app store. On a non-Huawei phone, download
   it from Huawei's official website,
   [appgallery.huawei.com](https://appgallery.huawei.com/), and install
   it (Android asks you to allow the installation from your browser).
2. **Huawei Health** (*Huawei Santé* in French), from AppGallery. It is
   the app that pairs and synchronizes your watch.
3. **HMS Core** (Huawei Mobile Services), from AppGallery. It is usually
   installed together with Huawei Health; check that it is present and
   up to date.
4. Sign in to Huawei Health with your **HUAWEI ID** (Huawei account),
   and check that your watch's steps appear in Huawei Health.

Then, once support is available, Step Challenge will ask for your
permission to read your steps from Huawei Health. Only the daily step
count will be read, as described in the
[privacy policy]({{< relref "privacy" >}}).
